We make backups, but the service does not replace your own. Regularly export whatever matters to you
with **Import / Export → all-draw JSON** (`.alldraw.json`): it is an open format that contains the
whole workspace and can be imported again here or into any all-draw installation. Local workspaces
exist only in your browser: if you lose them, we cannot recover them. See [history](historial.md).
